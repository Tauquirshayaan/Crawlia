# What is Swokei?

Swokei is an AI-powered cold outreach platform built for web agencies and freelancers who sell web design, SEO, and digital services. It finds prospects, audits their websites, writes personalized emails referencing their specific problems, sends them from your inbox, and automatically sorts replies so you know who's interested.

![Swokei dashboard overview](images/dashboard-overview.png)

## Why cold outreach usually fails

The traditional way to prospect is painful and doesn't scale. You spend hours hunting for leads, then you visit each website and try to spot things you could pitch. You write an email (usually generic), send it, and hope. Days later you follow up manually. When replies come in, you read every single one to figure out which prospects are actually interested. At real volume, this takes 10-15 hours every week.

Most importantly, generic emails don't work. Prospects get dozens of templated pitches every week. They delete them immediately. The ones who reply are often not a good fit — they're just being polite or curious.

## How Swokei changes the game

Instead of guessing what to pitch, Swokei audits each prospect's website first. The AI analyzes their design quality, performance, SEO, mobile experience, trust signals, and content — then writes an email that references real, specific issues the AI found. The prospect reads an email about their actual website, not a template about web design.

Response rates are higher. Follow-ups happen automatically. Replies get classified by AI so you only read the ones that matter. And the whole thing runs while you're doing other work.

## The workflow in six steps

## What you can do with Swokei

- Smart Outreach campaigns — upload leads, let Swokei audit their sites and write personalized emails, pay 1 credit per successful analysis
- General Outreach campaigns — write your own email template, Swokei handles sending and tracking, no credits used
- Email warmup — gradually ramp sending volume over weeks to build sender reputation before running full campaigns
- Lead Finder — discover agencies or service providers in specific cities and industries, then target them
- Follow-up automation — define your own sequence (timing, number of attempts, when to stop)
- Inbox management — all replies land in one place, auto-classified by AI, with notes and deal tracking on each lead
- Website analysis — see the 6-dimension breakdown for any lead so you know exactly what to pitch
- Campaign rules — skip sites below a certain score threshold, set sending time windows, define your own targeting logic
## Who should use Swokei

Swokei is built for:

- Freelance web designers and developers prospecting for new clients
- Web agencies running outbound campaigns to fill the pipeline
- SEO agencies pitching audits and optimization services
- Digital agencies targeting local business prospects
If you sell a service that improves websites — design, performance, SEO, mobile experience, trust, content — you can use Swokei to find the right people and pitch them with credible, research-backed emails.

## What Swokei is not

Swokei is not a mass-blast email tool. It's designed for high-quality outreach where every email is meaningfully personalized based on a real website audit. The goal is a response rate worth having — conversations that turn into real opportunities, not a volume play that destroys your sender reputation.

You're not sending 10,000 emails a month. You're sending 50–200 targeted, relevant emails to prospects who actually have a problem you can solve.
