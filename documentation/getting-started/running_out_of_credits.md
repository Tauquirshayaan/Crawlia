# Running Out of Credits

Credits run out mid-campaign sometimes. Here's exactly what happens, what stops, what keeps going, and how to get back up and running quickly.

![Billing page showing credit balance and plan](images/account-billing-clean.png)

## What happens when credits hit zero

When your credit balance reaches 0, Swokei stops generating new emails for unprocessed leads. Specifically:

- Stops: Website analysis and email generation for any leads that haven't been processed yet. These leads stay in the campaign in a pending state.
- Keeps going: Emails that have already been generated (status: Ready or already Sent) continue sending on schedule. Running out of credits doesn't cancel emails that are already in the queue.
- Keeps going: Follow-up emails for leads already in a sequence continue sending. Follow-ups don't cost credits — only the initial analysis and generation does.
- Keeps going: Reply detection and classification continue regardless of credit balance.
## How to check your credit balance

Your current balance is always visible in the top navigation bar of the app. For a detailed breakdown of recent usage, go to Account → Billing & plan → Credit history. This shows every credit event — which campaigns used credits, when, and how many — so you can see exactly where they went.

## How to get more credits immediately

- In the sidebar, click "Account".
- Click the "Billing" tab.
- Find the "Buy credits" or "Top up" section.
- Select a credit bundle size from the options shown.
- Click "Buy now" and complete the payment. Credits are added to your balance instantly — no waiting for a billing cycle.
- Return to your campaign. The leads that were stuck in pending will now be processed automatically.
## How to wait for your billing cycle to renew

If you don't want to buy a top-up, credits replenish automatically at the start of your next billing period. To see when that is:

- Go to Account → Billing & plan.
- Look for your next renewal date shown in the billing summary. Your monthly credit allocation is added to your balance on that date.
- Leads stuck in pending will be picked up and processed automatically once credits are available.
## How to upgrade to get more credits per month

If you regularly run out of credits before the end of the month, upgrading your plan is the better long-term fix:

- Go to Account → Billing & plan.
- Review the plan comparison table and find the plan with enough credits for your typical monthly volume.
- Click "Upgrade" next to that plan.
- The upgrade takes effect immediately and your new monthly allocation is credited right away.
## Do already-sent follow-ups keep going when credits hit zero?

Yes. Follow-up emails are not tied to credits. Once the initial email has been generated and sent (which is what uses the credit), all subsequent follow-ups in the sequence send for free, regardless of your credit balance. Running out of credits does not disrupt any active follow-up sequences.

## What happens to leads that were still pending when credits ran out?

They stay in the campaign in a pending state. As soon as credits are added back — whether via a top-up or the monthly billing cycle renewing — Swokei automatically picks up where it left off and processes those pending leads. You don't have to relaunch the campaign or manually trigger anything.

## Will Swokei warn me before I run out?

Yes. When your credit balance drops to a low level, Swokei sends an in-app notification and, if enabled, an email alert. You can control which alerts you receive in Account → Notifications. Enabling the "Low credit balance" notification is highly recommended if you run regular campaigns.

## How to prevent running out mid-campaign

- Check your balance before launching. If you have 50 credits and want to run 200 leads, top up first or wait for your billing cycle.
- Set a score threshold. In the campaign Rules step, set a minimum website score (e.g. 6). Leads with higher-scoring sites are skipped without using credits — this alone can reduce credit usage by 20–40% on a typical list.
- Estimate your usage first. Count your leads, subtract any you expect will be skipped (bad URLs, high-scoring sites), and compare to your current balance before launching.
- Use rollover strategically. A slow month where you run fewer campaigns means credits accumulate for the next month. If you know a heavy push is coming, hold back the month before.
- Monitor team usage. If workspace members are also running campaigns, their usage draws from the same pool. Check Account → Billing & plan → Credit history to see usage broken down by campaign.
## Is there a way to see how fast I'm burning credits?

Yes — the credit history in Account → Billing & plan shows every credit event with a timestamp, campaign name, and credit amount. Review it after each campaign to understand your typical burn rate. If you regularly use 400+ of 600 credits each month, the Pro plan (1,100 credits) will give you more headroom without needing top-ups.
