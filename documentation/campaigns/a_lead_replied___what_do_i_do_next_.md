# A Lead Replied — What Do I Do Next?

A reply arrived. Swokei has classified it and stopped further follow-ups. Now it's your move. Here's what to do depending on the type of reply, with templates for common scenarios.

## First, check the reply in Inbox

Go to "Inbox" in the sidebar. Unread replies are highlighted. Click on the reply to open the full thread. In the right-side CRM panel, you'll see Swokei's classification: Interested, Not interested, Question, Auto-reply, or Unsubscribe. If the classification is wrong, click the dropdown and correct it.

![Inbox with reply open showing the CRM panel on the right](images/inbox-with-reply.png)

## If they're interested: responding quickly matters

Interest fades. Reply within hours if possible. Here's how to handle the most common questions:

### "Tell me more" or "What does this involve?"

Keep it short and specific, not a brochure:

### "How much does this cost?"

Give a range, not a fixed quote. Committing to a number before understanding scope underprices you:

### "When can you start?" or "Let's book a call"

Make it frictionless with a calendar link or two specific times:

## After the first exchange: document it in CRM

Once you've had your first real conversation, record it in Swokei so you have a complete history:

- In the reply view, look at the CRM panel on the right.
- Set the Deal stage dropdown — "Call booked" if you've scheduled something, "Proposal sent" if you're sending a quote, "Contacted" if you're still early.
- Type notes about the conversation in the Notes field — what they said, their budget, timeline, concerns.
- If you discussed a rough figure, enter it in the Deal value field.
- If a call is booked, click "Add meeting", fill in title/date/time/link, and click "Save meeting".
Everything saves automatically. You'll find this lead later in the CRM view with full context intact.

## If they asked a question (engaged but not committed)

A question reply means they're interested enough to engage but haven't decided. Answer their specific question directly and propose one next step:

Then set the deal stage to "Contacted" and add a note with what they asked so you remember the context later.

## If they're not interested

Reply graciously with no pushback or hard sell:

Then in Swokei, verify the classification is set to "Not interested" and click "Mark as answered" to clear it from your active inbox. No further action needed — Swokei has already stopped follow-ups.

## If it's an auto-reply (out of office)

Read the auto-reply to see when they're back. Add a note: "Out of office until [date] — follow up when they return." If the follow-up sequence hasn't finished, let it continue and they'll get the next email when they're back. If the sequence is done, set a reminder in your calendar to manually check in when they return. Mark the reply as answered to clear it from your inbox.

## If they asked to unsubscribe

Swokei auto-stops follow-ups on unsubscribe requests. Verify the classification shows "Unsubscribe", then reply politely: "Of course — no problem at all. You won't hear from me again." Mark as answered.

## Ambiguous replies and what they usually mean

If Swokei marks a reply as Unclassified, read carefully and set the classification yourself. Common ambiguous replies and how to interpret them:

- "Who are you?" — Treat as Interested. They engaged and want context.
- "We already have someone for that" — Not interested. Move on.
- "Can you send more info?" — Interested. Respond quickly.
- "How much does this cost?" — Interested. They're evaluating, not rejecting.
- "Not right now" — Not interested. Treat as No, but consider re-targeting in 6 months.
## Replies to old emails in the sequence

Swokei syncs replies regardless of which email triggered them. Whether they reply to your initial email or a follow-up from weeks ago, it appears in the same reply thread in Inbox. Handle it exactly the same way — the reply is the signal, not which email in the sequence prompted it.
